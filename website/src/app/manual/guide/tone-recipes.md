These are starting points, not finished presets: a model, a cab and a handful of settings that put you in the right neighbourhood for a style. Load one, play, then adjust with the method in [Getting your sound](/manual/getting-your-sound). Your guitar, pickups and touch will move every number.

Each recipe names the Cathode amp models to try, whether a drive goes in front, the cab and mic, starting settings, and effects. Where the Factory bank has presets in the style, they are listed: they are complete, levelled versions of these ideas, and the quickest way to start is to load one and save your own copy.

Knob names follow each model's own front panel, so a Plexi shows **Treble Vol** where a Twin shows **Volume**. Values on 0 to 10 knobs are as shown on the knob. Cab mic **Position** is from 0 % (the cap's centre) to 100 % (the cone's edge), **Distance** in inches from the grille.

> **Tip:** Every recipe assumes Input 1 is calibrated (see [Gain staging](/manual/gain-staging#calibrate-input-1)) and the Amp's **Speaker load** is on **Follow Cab**. After building one, level it with **Preset Leveling** at -18 LUFS so it sits with the Factory bank.

## Blues

Touch-sensitive, mid-rich, breaking up when you dig in and cleaning up when you play softly or roll the guitar's volume back.

|              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Amps**     | **Bluesbreaker 1962**, **Tweed Deluxe 5E3 Jumped**, **Bassman 5F6-A Jumped**, **Vibroverb AB763**, **Super Reverb**, **Deluxe Reverb**, **JTM45**; for smooth leads, **ODS 70s** or **ODS 97**                                       |
| **Drive**    | Optional. A **TS808 OD** at **Drive** 1 to 2, **Tone** 6, **Level** +4 to +6 dB, for leads; a **Governor Dist** for a thicker British sound                                                                                          |
| **Cab**      | Fenders: **1x12 Open Back** or **4x10 Open Back** with a **Jensen C12N**, **Jensen P10R** or **Electro-Voice EVM12L**. Marshalls: **2x12 Open Back** or **4x12 Closed Back** with a **Greenback**. **SM57** at 25 to 35 %, 1 to 2 in |
| **Settings** | Gain (or **Volume**) just past the point of breakup on a hard strum: 5 to 7. Bass 3.5 to 4.5, Middle 6 to 7, Treble 6. On a Bluesbreaker, **Jumper** on and **Volume II** around 4 to 5                                              |
| **Effects**  | **Spring** reverb on Fenders (**Mix** 15 to 20 %), a short **Room** on Marshalls; an **Analog** delay of 350 to 450 ms at low mix for leads                                                                                          |

Factory presets: "Texas Flood", "Lenny", "The Thrill Is Gone", "Hideaway", "Still Got the Blues", "Talk to Your Daughter", "Slow Dancing in a Burning Room", "Ice Pickin'".

## Classic rock

Loud, open crunch from amps without a master volume, where the power amp does much of the work.

|              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amps**     | **Plexi 100W**, **1959SLP**, **1987X**, **JTM45**, **Plexi**; **AC30** for the British invasion; **Hiwatt DR103** for big, clean-ish power chords; **Major 1967**; **Tweed Deluxe 5E3 Jumped** for raw garage crunch                  |
| **Drive**    | None, or a **Treble Booster** (**Drive** 5 to 8) in front for 1970s leads. **Face Fuzz** for late-1960s fuzz                                                                                                                          |
| **Cab**      | **4x12 Closed Back** with **Greenback** or **G12H30 Anniversary**; **2x12 Open Back** with **Alnico Blue** for the AC30. **SM57** at 20 to 30 %, 1 in                                                                                 |
| **Settings** | Marshalls: **Jumper** on, **Treble Vol** 6 to 8, **Normal Vol** 4 to 5, Bass 5, Middle 6 to 7, Treble 6 to 7, Presence 5. AC30: **Gain** 6 to 8, **Cut** 4 to 5. Hiwatt: **Brilliant Vol** 7, **Normal Vol** 4 to 6, **Master Vol** 8 |
| **Effects**  | A short **Room** reverb (**Mix** about 10 %); a **Tape** delay around 280 to 380 ms for solos; a **Rotary** or **Vibe** for late-1960s sounds                                                                                         |

Factory presets: "Purple Haze", "Little Wing", "Machine Gun", "Sunshine of Your Love", "Whole Lotta Love", "Smoke on the Water", "Baba O'Riley", "Comfortably Numb Solo", "Cinnamon Girl", "Layla", "Bohemian Rhapsody Solo", "Day Tripper", "Sultans of Swing", "Money for Nothing", "More Than a Feeling", "Back in Black".

> **Tip:** A cranked non-master amp cleans up beautifully with the guitar's volume knob. Set the amp for your loudest part and play the rest with the knob.

## Hard rock

More gain than classic rock, from master-volume amps or hot-rodded Marshalls, usually with a boost.

|              |                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amps**     | **JCM800 2203** or **JCM800 2204** with a boost; **SLO-100**; **AFD100**; **Silver Jubilee**; **5150**; **JVM410H OD**; **DSL100**; **BE-100**; **Legacy VL100** for smooth 1980s leads                       |
| **Drive**    | **TS9 OD** or **TS808 OD** at **Drive** 0 to 2, **Tone** 6, **Level** +5 to +6 dB, as a [boost](/manual/signal-chain#the-drive-as-a-boost). Or a **Plus Distortion** or **DS1 Distortion** into a crunchy amp |
| **Cab**      | **4x12 Closed Back**, **G12T-75** (1980s) or **Vintage 30**. **SM57** at 20 to 25 %, 1 in                                                                                                                     |
| **Settings** | JCM800: **Preamp** 7 to 8, **Master** 5, Bass 5, Middle 6, Treble 7, Presence 6. SLO-100: **Gain** 7, **Depth** 5, **Master** 5                                                                               |
| **Effects**  | **Digital** or **Analog** delay at 350 to 420 ms, **Mix** 15 %, on the solo scene; a **Plate** or **Hall** reverb at low mix                                                                                  |

For a "brown" late-1970s sound, take a **Plexi 100W** with everything up and lower its **Variac** (Power Amp page) to about 85 %: the lower mains voltage makes the amp spongier and smoother.

Factory presets: "Brown Sound", "Sweet Child O' Mine", "Crazy Train", "Still of the Night", "Surfing with the Alien", "Cliffs of Dover", "Building the Church", "Poundcake", "Don't Tell Me (What Love Can Do)", "Tom Sawyer", "Paranoid", "Breaking the Law".

## Modern metal and djent

Tight, articulate, saturated rhythm, with a dead-silent stop between chugs.

|              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amps**     | **5150III**, **5150**, **Dual Rectifier** (with **Modern** on), **Triple Rectifier**, **Mark IIC+**, **Mark V**, **Powerball**, **Savage 120**, **Prometheus**, **BE-100 HBE**                                                                              |
| **Drive**    | Almost always: **TS9 OD** or **TS808 OD**, **Drive** 0, **Tone** 6, **Level** +6 dB. This is what tightens the low end                                                                                                                                      |
| **Gate**     | **Input** gate on **Intelligent**, **Threshold** -50 to -55 dB. For the tightest stops, also a **Gate/Expander** after the cab with **Sidechain** on **Grid Input**                                                                                         |
| **Cab**      | **4x12 Closed Back**, **Vintage 30**. **SM57** close: **Position** 15 to 25 %, **Distance** 0.5 to 1 in. Blend an **R-121** 4 dB lower for weight                                                                                                           |
| **Settings** | Gain lower than you think: 5 to 6 on a 5150III. Bass 4 to 6, Middle 5 to 6, Treble 6, Presence 6, **Resonance** (Depth) 4 to 5, **Master** or **Volume** 5. On a Mark, the graphic EQ in a gentle V (80 Hz +2, 240 Hz 0, 750 Hz -3, 2200 Hz +1, 6600 Hz +2) |
| **Cuts**     | Cab **Low Cut** 80 to 100 Hz, **High Cut** 8 to 10 kHz. The amp's **Low Cut** on its Preamp page (60 to 100 Hz) tightens further before the distortion                                                                                                      |
| **Effects**  | None on the rhythm. Delay and a plate on the lead scene                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

For extended-range guitars and djent, take low end out **before** the amp (the boost, the amp's **Low Cut**, or **Input Cut**) rather than after it, and cut a little around 500 Hz after the cab if it boxes up. Keep the mids: a deep scoop vanishes in a mix.

Factory presets: "Master of Puppets", "Enter Sandman Rhythm", "Du Hast", "Djent Rhythm", "Ghost of Perdition", "The Enemy Inside", "Schism", "Killing in the Name".

## Clean and funk

Clean, bright and percussive, with every note clear.

|              |                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amps**     | **Twin Reverb**, **Bandmaster AB763**, **Deluxe Reverb**, **Super Reverb**, **Blackface**; **JC-120** for glassy, effect-heavy cleans; **Hiwatt DR103** at low volume                           |
| **Drive**    | None                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Dynamics** | A **Compressor** before the amp, **Type** Pedal, **Input Level** Instrument: **Threshold** about -32 dB, **Ratio** 4 to 6, **Attack** 3 to 5 ms, **Release** 120 to 150 ms, **Makeup** to match |
| **Cab**      | **2x12 Open Back** with **JBL D120F** for sparkle, or **Jensen C12N**. **SM57** at 20 to 30 %, 1 in                                                                                             |
| **Settings** | **Volume** 3.5 to 4.5 (clean with headroom), Bass 3 to 4, Middle 4 to 5, Treble 6 to 7, **Bright** on                                                                                           |
| **Effects**  | A short **Room** reverb at low mix; a **Wah** for 1970s funk rhythm; a **Chorus** for 1980s cleans                                                                                              |

Clean presets clip the outputs more easily than distorted ones. A compressor after the cab with a high threshold and high ratio (as in the Factory presets) catches the peaks.

Factory presets: "Le Freak", "Theme from Shaft", "Can't Stop", "Fade to Black Intro", "Message in a Bottle", "Creep".

## Ambient and shoegaze

Washes of sound: long reverbs, modulated delays, swells and fuzz.

|              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amps**     | **AC30**, **Deluxe Reverb**, **JC-120**, **Twin Reverb**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Drive**    | For shoegaze, a **Pi Fuzz** (**Drive** 7 to 8) or a **Blues OD** in front. None for ambient cleans                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Cab**      | **2x12 Open Back** with **Alnico Blue** or **JBL D120F**, or **1x12 Open Back** with **Jensen C12N**. **SM57** at 30 to 35 %, 2 in                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Settings** | AC30: **Gain** 1 to 6 (clean to light breakup), **Cut** 4 to 5. Deluxe Reverb: **Volume** 5                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Effects**  | **Chorus** (slow **Rate**, 0.3 to 0.8 Hz). A **Digital** or **Dual** delay at 400 to 700 ms, **Feedback** 40 to 50 %, **Mix** about 30 %, with the **Ducker** on so it blooms between phrases. **Cathedral** or **Hall** reverb, **Decay Time** 4 to 8 s, **Mix** 30 to 40 %. **Reverse** delay for backwards textures. A **Volume/Pan** block on **Auto-Swell** for violin-like swells |

Put the delay and reverb on parallel rows with **Kill Dry** on for a cleaner wash, and set their **Bypass mode** to **Mute FX In (spillover)** so the tails ring over scene changes (see [Building a signal chain](/manual/signal-chain#spillover)).

Factory presets: "Only Shallow", "When the Sun Hits", "Heaven or Las Vegas", "Svefn-g-englar", "Where the Streets Have No Name", "How Soon Is Now".

## Lead tones

A lead has to sit on top of the band: more sustain, more mids, a little louder, and some space around it.

|              |                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amps**     | **Mark IIC+** or **Mark IV** (raise **Lead Drive**); **SLO-100**; **ODS 70s** or **ODS 97** with **Overdrive** on; a **Plexi 100W** or **1987X** with a boost; **Legacy VL100**; **Silver Jubilee** |
| **Drive**    | A boost (**TS808 OD**, **Treble Booster**, **Chiron OD**) switched on by the lead scene                                                                                                             |
| **Cab**      | As for the rhythm sound, but try the mic a little further from the cap for a smoother top                                                                                                           |
| **Settings** | Middle up a step from the rhythm sound; Presence a touch lower if the top gets spiky                                                                                                                |
| **Effects**  | Delay at 350 to 450 ms, **Feedback** 20 to 30 %, **Mix** 15 to 20 %, with the **Ducker**; a **Hall** or **Plate** at low mix                                                                        |
| **Level**    | Raise the lead scene's **Scene Level** (Controllers page) 1 to 3 dB above the rhythm                                                                                                                |

Build the rhythm and lead as two scenes of one preset: the lead scene engages the boost and the delay and raises the level, and with spillover the delay tail survives the switch back. Many Factory presets have exactly this rhythm and lead pair.

## Where to go from here

- Swap the speaker or the mic position before you change the amp: it is often the missing piece.
- Try the same recipe on the neighbouring models in the family: a **1987X** instead of a **1959SLP**, a **Dual Rectifier 3-Ch** instead of a **Dual Rectifier**.
- Use a NAM capture of your own amp in place of the circuit model, with the same cab and effects: see [Neural captures and impulse responses](/manual/nam-and-irs).
